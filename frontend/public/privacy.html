Privacy Policy

Betgistics (betgistics.com)
Effective Date: November 3, 2026
Last Updated: November 3, 2026
Version: 2.0.0

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Betgistics ("Betgistics," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the Betgistics website and app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What Betgistics is — and is not. Betgistics is a sports research and bet-tracking tool. It is not a sportsbook. Betgistics does not take bets, does not build or place parlays, and does not hold, accept or transfer money on your behalf. Any wager you make is placed with a third-party sportsbook, not with Betgistics.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Google sign-in account information

If you sign in, you do so with your Google account. Google shares the basic profile information you approve, which we store to identify your account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • Your Google account identifier

We never receive or store your Google password.

2.2 Bets you log (straight bets only)

When you choose to log a bet in Bet History, we store the details you enter or carry over from the calculators so you can track your results. The bet log supports straight bets only (spread, total or moneyline on a single game). A logged bet can include:

  • Sport, teams and the team statistics used in your analysis
  • The line, odds, and the probability and projected margin shown by the calculators
  • The bankroll, stake and Kelly percentage figures you entered
  • The outcome you record (win, loss or push) and any notes

These are records you type in for your own tracking. Logging a bet does not place a wager anywhere.

2.3 Usage needed to run the service

  • A count of the probability calculations used by your account
  • Standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and request time) used for security and troubleshooting
  • Preferences stored in your own browser (for example your theme, last-opened tab and any media you upload to the Media page, which stays on your device)

2.4 Calculator explanations

The Kelly Calculator can show a short written explanation of your result. To generate it, only the numbers from that calculation (bankroll, odds, probability and recommended stake) are sent to a third-party AI service provider. Your name, email and bet log are not included.

2.5 Payments

If you choose an optional paid plan, payment is handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number. We keep only the subscription status Stripe reports back so we can unlock access.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To show your logged bets, results and bankroll history back to you
  • To operate, maintain and fix the service
  • To respond when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. How Information Is Shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run Betgistics, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Google — sign-in (authentication)
  • Our hosting and database providers — to store and serve your account and bet log
  • An AI service provider — only the calculation numbers described in section 2.4
  • Stripe — only if you choose a paid plan

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the service.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account information and bet log for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information and logged bets, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view your logged bets and update their outcomes in Bet History.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual bets in Bet History, or email us to delete your whole account.
  • Sign out: you can sign out at any time and revoke Betgistics' access from your Google account settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your data. Email us to exercise them; we respond within 30 days.

7. Age Restriction

Betgistics is for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls on our database, and sign-in through Google rather than our own passwords. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Disclaimer

Betgistics is provided for informational and educational purposes only. We make no guarantee of the accuracy of any calculation, probability, projection or prediction shown in the app. Do not rely solely on Betgistics calculations when deciding whether to place a bet.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date and version above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
